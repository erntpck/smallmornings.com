A note on health data. Records such as medications, illnesses, vaccinations, symptoms, mood and EPDS are special category data under the law. As long as they stay on your phone, only you can see them; Small Mornings cannot access them.

Chat history. Your conversations with the assistant are stored only on your phone; there is no copy on the server. You can clear the chat inside the app.

On restore, the file you pick is first copied into the app's cache folder, then read, validated and, after your confirmation, written over the records on the phone; that copy is subject to the same rules as the cached backup copy.

Section 5

Data sent to the server

The app goes online in exactly three situations. It makes no other network requests. This web page also has connections of its own (5.4).

5.1 Assistant

When. After you tap "Accept and start" on the consent card in the Assistant tab, and only when you send a message. Before you consent, no assistant-related network request is made. Your consent is stored on your phone.

What is sent. Each message carries three parts:

  1. The new message you wrote.
  2. Up to the last 20 messages of the same chat (so the assistant remembers the topic).
  3. A context summary the app builds from your records at that moment (about 7,000 characters at most). This summary may contain:
    • Your child's name, age in months (or pregnancy week) and gender if entered. The date of birth itself is not sent.
    • The latest growth measurement (weight, length, head circumference and date).
    • The last 3 sleep records and today's feedings (last 10); the average sleep duration and number of meals over the last 7 days.
    • The last 5 completed milestones, those you marked as not yet reached, and upcoming ones.
    • Up to 5 active medications (name, dose, interval, end date); chronic conditions and permanent medications (name, date and the first 100 characters of notes).
    • The next 3 appointments (title, date, time, doctor name and place); the last 5 past appointments (date, title, type, doctor name and the first 100 characters of notes); the last 5 illness records (date, title, doctor name, first 100 characters of notes).
    • Vaccinations: done, marked as refused, upcoming and next; upcoming and overdue doctor checkups.
    • Teeth: erupted, marked as not yet, and the next expected tooth.
    • Mom's entry for today: mood, symptoms, water, exercise and weekly average sleep.
    If the summary exceeds the limit, lower-priority parts (for example past appointments) are dropped entirely.

Never sent. Diaper records, the mom journal, EPDS results, period, weight and recovery records, mom appointments and reminders, the shopping list, tasks, notification settings and other app settings are never sent to the assistant.

Where it goes. The request travels over HTTPS to the Small Mornings server, which the developer runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare, Inc. is a US company and the request is handled at the point of its global network nearest to you. The server validates the request, forwards the same content to the Claude API of Anthropic PBC (USA) (model: Claude Haiku 5.5) and returns the reply to you. The request also carries an app token embedded in the app and your install id; the token shows the request comes from the Small Mornings app, it does not identify you.

Anthropic Claude. The assistant's AI model provider is Anthropic PBC (USA). Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through the API (your message, the chat history and the context summary) and the replies generated are not used to train Anthropic's models. Anthropic deletes these inputs and outputs within 30 days of receiving or generating them. Exceptions: where a different retention arrangement has been agreed (for example a zero data retention agreement), where content is flagged for a suspected violation of Anthropic's Usage Policy (kept for up to 2 years), or where a legal obligation requires longer retention.

Our advice. The context summary includes your child's name. If you prefer, use a nickname instead of the real name in the profile. Do not write identifying details such as surname, address, national id number or phone number in your messages. You may also never turn the assistant on; every other feature of the app works without it.

What stays on the server. Nothing. The server code neither stores nor logs your message, the context summary or the reply. The reply is written only to your phone.

What is logged. Only on error paths, and only this: the HTTP status code, error type and request ID returned by Anthropic; timeouts; empty replies; the model's reason for refusing or stopping a reply; read and write errors of the counter store (KV). These records never contain your message, the context summary, your install id or your IP address. Connection records kept by Cloudflare at platform level are subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.

IP address and request limit. As with any internet request, Cloudflare sees your IP address. To limit abuse, the server allows at most 20 requests per minute per IP address; this counter lives in server memory, resets within a minute and is never written to storage.

How to withdraw. Turn sharing off from the menu on the chat screen. After that no new request is sent; the chat history on your phone stays until you delete it. If you want to consent again, the card is shown again.

Legal basis. Because the summary may contain health data, this sharing happens only with your explicit consent under KVKK Art. 6/2 and GDPR Art. 9(2)(a); the transfer abroad is explained in section 6.

5.2 Monthly message allowance counter

The assistant has a monthly message limit. For this the server keeps only a number in Cloudflare KV under the key quota:<year-month>:<install id>; no message content is stored. The record deletes itself 40 days after it was last written. Because the install id is random, this counter cannot be matched to you. If the install id could not be attached to a request, the server keeps the counter by your IP address instead, for the same 40 days; the current app always sends the id, so this is only a fallback.

After you consent, when the chat screen opens the app asks the server with your install id how much allowance remains, without increasing the counter; this query runs at most once every 10 minutes. The counter is kept on Cloudflare servers that are also located outside Türkiye; we cannot choose the country in which the data is processed.

5.3 Vaccination and checkup schedule

The app downloads the current version of the vaccination and doctor checkup schedule from the Small Mornings server. This request carries no identity, token or install id; it only asks for the public schedule file. It runs at most once a day (when the app opens and 24 hours have passed) and when you refresh the schedule manually from the vaccination screen. The reply is cached on your phone. The server keeps no records for these requests; your IP address is seen by Cloudflare only for the duration of the connection.

5.4 This web page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) and is also served from the Small Mornings server as a mirror. GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of your visit (IP address, browser information, time) under the GitHub privacy statement. The page loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request with your IP address to Google's servers. The page has no cookies, analytics or tracking code, and runs no JavaScript. If you open the page from the Small Mornings server (api.smallmornings.com/privacy/en), Cloudflare rather than GitHub sees your IP address; the Google Fonts request is unchanged.

Section 9

Children's data and parental responsibility

Small Mornings is for adults (18 and over): parents and caregivers. You enter your child's information as their legal guardian. That information stays on your phone and is sent to the assistant only if you give the consent in section 5.1, within the scope described there.

The app is not designed for children to use themselves and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. For this reason the US COPPA rules, Apple's "Kids" category and Google Play's "Designed for Families" program do not apply to this app.

If you believe a child's data was entered by someone without authority to do so, write to erentepecik@erentepecik.com. Because the data lives on the phone, deletion is done from that phone; we will work out how together.

If you want to keep your child's data to a minimum, you can use a nickname instead of the real name in the profile; no feature of the app depends on the real name.

Section 10

Notifications

Reminders (medication time, appointments, vaccinations, checkups, mom reminders and info cards) are scheduled locally on your phone. There is no notification server, push token or remote notification; notification content never leaves your phone.

In Settings you can turn notification categories off one by one and set quiet hours. You can revoke notification permission at any time in the operating system settings; the app respects this and schedules nothing without permission. On Android the app uses only the permissions notifications need, plus internet access: showing notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), exact alarms so reminders fire at the exact time (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), re-creating reminders after the phone restarts (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), vibration and internet (INTERNET). The toolchain the app is built with may declare some standard permissions aimed at older Android versions (for example the legacy storage permission) in the manifest by default; the app does not use them and never asks you for them. No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo permission is requested.

Section 11

Security and data breaches

What we do. All communication with the server is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS). The server accepts assistant and counter requests only with the app token (the public schedule file and this page need no token), limits request size and applies a per-IP request limit. The key for accessing Anthropic lives only on the server and is not embedded in the app. Because no content is stored on the server and there are no accounts, there is no user database that could leak. These measures are applied with regard to the adequate measures the Board set for special-category data in decision 2018/10 of 31 January 2018: special-category data is transmitted only encrypted with TLS, is not stored on the server, and only the developer has access to the server and its keys.

What we ask of you. Your data sits in the app's storage on your phone, protected by the operating system's app isolation; it is not separately encrypted. The real protection is your phone's screen lock; please use a passcode or biometric lock. iOS and Android device backups (iCloud, Google One and similar) may include app data; those backups are governed by Apple's and Google's own policies and are outside the control of Small Mornings. Backup files are plain text; keep them somewhere you trust and take care when sharing them.

Honest limits. The app token is not a personal secret, because it is embedded in the app; its purpose is to filter casual abuse. Processing of content that reaches Anthropic is subject to Anthropic's security practices. No system is completely secure, and we do not promise that.

If a breach happens. If we learn that your personal data has been obtained unlawfully, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Board within 72 hours at the latest, as required by KVKK Art. 12/5 and the Board's decision, and inform affected persons as soon as reasonably possible. Where GDPR applies, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours (Art. 33) and inform you directly in high-risk cases (Art. 34). Because there are no accounts, in most cases we can inform you only through this page and an in-app notice. If you spot a security weakness, please write to erentepecik@erentepecik.com.

Section 13

Automated decisions and artificial intelligence

Assistant replies are generated by an AI model (Anthropic Claude). They may be wrong, incomplete or out of date; they are not medical advice (section 17). The model may refuse to answer some messages; the app then shows you a notice.

Small Mornings makes no automated decision about you or your child that has legal effect or similarly significant impact (KVKK Art. 11/1-g, GDPR Art. 22). The assistant is a suggestion tool; decisions are made by you and your doctor.

There is no profiling for advertising or marketing. The stage cards and reminders inside the app are computed locally from the records on your phone and your child's age; they never go to the server.

Section 14

Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed.
  • Request information if it has been processed.
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with it.
  • Know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom it has been transferred.
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate.
  • Request erasure or destruction under the conditions set by law.
  • Request that corrections, erasures and destructions be notified to third parties that received the data.
  • Object to a result that is against you and arises solely from automated analysis.
  • Claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Where GDPR applies you also have the right of access and to a copy (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), withdrawal of consent at any time (Art. 7(3)) and not to be subject to solely automated decisions (Art. 22).

How to exercise them. Because Small Mornings keeps no data on the server, you exercise most of these rights directly on your phone:

  • View and correct: in the relevant screens.
  • Copy and portability: "Create backup" under "Your data" on the Profile screen gives you all of your data as a single JSON file.
  • Erasure: "Delete profile" on the Profile screen, or uninstalling the app (section 15).
  • Withdraw consent: turn sharing off in the chat menu.
  • Everything else: by e-mail to erentepecik@erentepecik.com.

Identity verification. There are no accounts, so we hold no record that identifies you. Under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, your application must contain your name and surname (and a signature if made in writing), Turkish ID number (for foreign nationals, nationality and passport or ID number), an address for service, an e-mail address if you have one, and the subject of the request; you may apply in Turkish and also in writing rather than by e-mail. Because there are no accounts, we also accept applications from your ordinary e-mail address; we do not ask for a copy of an identity document or any further information. The only record on the server is the counter tied to the anonymous install id; that id is not shown in the app, so we may not be able to match a specific counter to you (section 15).

Time and cost. We answer requests as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest. Where GDPR applies the period is one month; for complex or numerous requests it may be extended by two further months, and you will be told of the extension and its reasons (Art. 12(3)). Requests are free of charge; in the exceptional cases allowed by law, the fee schedule set by the Board may apply.

Complaints. Under KVKK Art. 14, if your application is refused, you find the answer insufficient or you receive no answer within 30 days, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr) within 30 days of learning the answer and in any case within 60 days of your application. A complaint to the Board requires that you first applied to us. Where GDPR applies, you may complain to the data protection authority of the EU member state where you live or work, or to the ICO in the United Kingdom; you do not need to contact us first.

Section 15

Deleting your data

Deleting inside the app. "Delete profile" on the Profile screen does the following, in order:

  1. Cancels every scheduled notification.
  2. Deletes all Small Mornings data on the phone: the profile, baby and mom records, chat history, assistant consent, install id, message allowance cache, review prompt counters and the first-launch date.
  3. Only three things remain in the app's data store: your language preference, the cached vaccination schedule and the schedule version you have seen. None of these contains personal data.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything on the phone, including the cache folder.

What remains after deletion.

  • The monthly counter on the server: when the profile is deleted the install id is removed from the phone; the app generates a new id the next time you use the assistant and the old counter is never used again. The old counter deletes itself within 40 days at most.
  • Backup files you made yourself: they stay wherever you saved them; you delete them.
  • Backup copies in the app's cache folder (the backup you created and the file you picked for a restore): "Delete profile" does not touch them; they remain until the operating system clears them or the app is uninstalled.
  • Your phone's device backups (iCloud, Google One and similar): governed by Apple's and Google's policies; you delete from there in your own settings.
  • Content that has reached Anthropic: governed by Anthropic's terms and deleted within 30 days at most (exceptions in 5.1); we cannot delete it separately.

Asking for the counter to be deleted early. Requests are handled by e-mail. Because we hold no identity data and the install id is not shown in the app, we may be unable to locate a specific counter without the id; in that case the counter deletes itself at the end of the 40-day period. The counter is only a number and cannot be matched to you.
