Section 2

The service and medical disclaimer

Small Mornings is a local-first app for keeping your baby's and your own daily records (feeding, sleep, diapers, growth, development, vaccinations, medications, checkups, appointments, maternal health) on your phone, setting reminders and reading general information cards. It needs no account; your records stay on your phone.

2.1 Informational only, not medical advice

The info cards, stage descriptions, averages, percentile charts, vaccination and checkup schedule and assistant replies in the app are for general information only; they do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and do not create a doctor-patient relationship. Consult your doctor for every decision about your child's or your own health. The app is not a medical device and is not designed to be classified as one. The EPDS test is a screening tool and does not diagnose; whatever the result, if you are not feeling well, see a health professional.

In an emergency do not use the app: call 112 (or your local emergency number) or go to the nearest emergency department. The emergency guide in the app gives general information on what to do until help arrives; it does not replace professional help.

2.2 Vaccination and checkup schedule

The schedule is compiled from the National Childhood Immunisation Schedule and follow-up programmes of the Turkish Ministry of Health; the source date is shown in the app. When the Ministry changes the schedule, the version in the app may lag behind. For the timing of vaccinations and checkups, what your family doctor or paediatrician says always prevails.

2.3 Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone and depend on your phone's settings, battery management, notification permission and the state of your subscription (section 5). Do not rely on the app alone for a critical medication, vaccination or appointment; note important dates separately as well.

When the trial or subscription ends (cancellation, failed payment, refund, or a trial that did not convert into a paid subscription):

  • None of your records are deleted. All of your records stay on your phone and remain visible, read-only, on the relevant screens.
  • Adding new records and editing in Plus tools stop. You can still delete a record, make a backup and delete the profile.
  • Insights such as charts, percentiles, averages and predictions stop.
  • Vaccination, checkup, medication and other Plus reminders, and info notifications, stop. The app says so clearly before the trial ends and when it ends; we recommend noting important vaccination and medication dates in your own calendar.

When you subscribe again or restore the subscription, everything opens again where it left off.

The app learns that the subscription has ended from Apple. When there is no internet connection or no answer from Apple, the app keeps the last known state for a short time (at most 72 hours); during that time your reminders are not cancelled by mistake.
